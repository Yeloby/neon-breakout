## Description

Break neon bricks, build combos and collect playful emoji power-ups in a
colorful arcade game from Yeloby Games.

Choose from three difficulty modes, explore varied brick formations and change
the action with multiball, shields, magnets, fireballs and more. Play with a
keyboard, mouse, touchpad or gamepad.

Neon Breakout is free, contains no advertising or in-app purchases, and can be
played offline. The interface is available in English and Norwegian.
